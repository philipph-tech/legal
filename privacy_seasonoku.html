Privacy Policy for Seasonoku

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how personal data is processed when you use the mobile game Seasonoku (the "App") on iOS and Android. In short: the App has no user accounts and no servers of my own. Your game progress stays on your device. Personal data is only processed by the third-party services described below, namely for advertising (Google AdMob) and for in-app purchases (Apple App Store, Google Play and RevenueCat).

1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

Philipp Hennig
Schirmerstraße 20

Email: phil.ventureshub@gmail.com

2. Data stored only on your device

The App saves the following information locally on your device so that you can continue playing where you left off:

  • game progress (unlocked levels, the current board and daily puzzle state, play time),
  • in-game currency and items (hearts, hints, reveals, the heart booster),
  • settings (e.g. vibrations, gold border).

This data is not transmitted to me and I have no access to it. It is deleted when you uninstall the App. If you have enabled device backups (e.g. iCloud or Google backup), this data may be included in your backup; that backup is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

The App does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos. The App does not use analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own.

3. Advertising with Google AdMob

The App shows banner ads and optional rewarded ads (e.g. to receive a hint). For this I use Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google").

Data processed

When ads are loaded and shown, Google may process in particular:

  • the advertising identifier of your device (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), if available and permitted,
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it,
  • device information (e.g. device model, operating system and version, language, screen size),
  • app information (e.g. app ID, app version),
  • information about ad interactions (e.g. ads shown, clicks, completed rewarded ads).

Purposes and legal basis

Advertising lets me offer the App free of charge. Before ads are loaded, the App asks for your consent where required by law (in particular in the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland) using Google's consent management tool (Google User Messaging Platform, based on the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework).

  • With your consent, information may be stored on and read from your device and Google may show you personalised ads based on your interests and measure their performance (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG).
  • Without your consent, Google may show non-personalised or limited ads. In that case Google may still process certain data (e.g. IP address, device information) to deliver ads, limit how often an ad is shown, and detect fraud and abuse. This is based on my legitimate interest in financing the App through advertising (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, where applicable, on § 25(2) TDDDG.

On iOS, the App additionally asks for permission to track via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared with Google. All game features remain available either way.

Changing your choices

  • Where consent is required, you can change or withdraw it at any time in the App under Options → Privacy options. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

If you purchase the heart booster, the banner ad is removed. Optional rewarded ads remain available.

Transfer to third countries

Google may transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and the European Commission has issued an adequacy decision for certified companies (Art. 45 GDPR). Google also uses the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

More information: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, How Google uses information for advertising.

4. In-app purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases. Payments are processed exclusively by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). I do not receive your payment details, name or email address from these purchases. The privacy policies of Apple and Google apply to the payment process.

To verify purchases, unlock purchased content and let you restore purchases (e.g. after reinstalling the App or on a new device), I use RevenueCat, a service of RevenueCat, Inc., USA. For this, RevenueCat processes:

  • a random, anonymous user ID generated by the App (not linked to your name or email),
  • purchase and transaction information from the App Store or Google Play (e.g. product, price, currency, purchase date, transaction ID, store country),
  • device and app information (e.g. operating system and version, app version, language),
  • your IP address.

The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and my legitimate interest in preventing fraud (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be processed in the USA; the transfer is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. RevenueCat acts as my processor under a data processing agreement. More information: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. App stores

When you download the App, the necessary information (e.g. your account, device and time of download) is transferred to Apple or Google. I have no influence on this processing. As the publisher, I may receive aggregated, anonymous statistics (e.g. number of installs) and, if you have agreed to share them with developers, crash reports from Apple or Google.

6. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 16. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Storage period

Data stored on your device remains there until you uninstall the App. Data processed by Google and RevenueCat is stored for as long as necessary for the purposes described above and in accordance with their privacy policies and statutory retention obligations.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR),
  • withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

To exercise your rights, contact me using the details in section 1. Because the App has no accounts, I usually cannot identify you; for data held by Google or RevenueCat, please include any identifier you can provide (e.g. a purchase transaction ID) so I can help.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, your place of work or the place of the alleged infringement (Art. 77 GDPR).

I do not use automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

9. Changes to this policy

I may update this privacy policy when the App or the law changes. The current version is always available on this page.
